Task 1. HTML5 Validation

Built-in browser validation: required, type, maxlength, pattern

Form with HTML5 Validation

How to test
  • Press Send with empty fields: the browser shows "Please fill out this field".
  • Type an email without @: the browser says the email is invalid.
  • Type 1 letter in Name: minlength blocks the form.
  • Try age 10 or 150: min and max block the form.
  • Try to type more than 200 characters in Message: maxlength stops you.
